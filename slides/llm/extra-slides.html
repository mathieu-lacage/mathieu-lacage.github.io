Mixture of Experts

The FFN of the previous slides: every token goes through the same Up and Down

Create 8 independent FFNs, each with its own weights: the experts

A router: x · Wrouter, then softmax: 8 scores per token

« Paris » keeps its top 2 scores: expert 3 (0.62) and expert 6 (0.38)

« Paris » goes through FFN 3 and FFN 6 only: the other 6 are not computed

« is »: its top 2 are experts 1 and 6, then the same multiply and sum

« the »: experts 1 and 4

« capital »: experts 6 and 8. Each token does the work of 2 FFNs, out of 8 stored

An expert is just a copy of the FFN of the previous slides (Up → wide vector → Down), with its own weights. The router picks 2 of the 8 per token; the other 6 are never computed for that token. The routing here is a cartoon. Qwen3-30B-A3B (local config.json): d = 2 048, 128 experts of d_ff = 768, 8 per token, 48 layers.

The router

« Paris »: its vector x, d numbers, as it enters the FFN layer

The router is one matrix, Wrouter: d rows, one column per expert

Score of expert 1: x · column 1, a dot product

Score of expert 2: x · column 2

And so on: x Wrouter gives 8 scores, one per expert

Softmax: the 8 scores become probabilities that sum to 1

Keep the top 2: expert 3 (0.41) and expert 6 (0.25). The other 6 are dropped

Renormalize the 2 kept: 0.41 / 0.66 = 0.62 and 0.25 / 0.66 = 0.38

Grouped-Query Attention

Same layer, H = 4 query heads, but only nkv = 2 key / value heads

WQ is still d × d, but WK and WV are only d × nkv dh: fewer weights, smaller K and V

Head 1 uses its slice of WQ, and slice 1 of WK and WV

Regroup by key / value head: heads 1 and 2 share K1, V1; heads 3 and 4 share K2, V2

Each query head still has its own attention: A1 = softmax(Q1 K1T …), A2 = softmax(Q2 K1T …): same keys, different queries

Z1 = A1 · V1 and Z2 = A2 · V1: the same values, mixed differently

From here on, nothing changes: the Zh form Z, n × d

WO is d × d, cut into H row slices

Head 1 adds Z1 · WO(1) to O

Head 2 adds Z2 · WO(2)

Head 3 adds Z3 · WO(3)

Head 4 adds Z4 · WO(4): O = Z · WO, n × d, as in multi-head attention

Grouped-query attention (GQA): n_q query heads share n_kv key / value heads, n_q / n_kv query heads per group. n_kv = n_q is multi-head attention, n_kv = 1 multi-query attention (MQA). Qwen3-1.7B: 16 query heads, 8 KV heads, the same 2 per group as here; Llama-2-7B is plain MHA. Why: K and V are what decoding keeps for every past token (the KV cache, in « Inference »); halving the KV heads halves the cache, at a small cost in quality.
